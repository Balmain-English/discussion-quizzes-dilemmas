Useful Vocabulary for Your Discussion

Hypnotize (verb)To put someone into a state of deep focus and relaxation where they respond strongly to suggestions.
Trance (noun)A half-awake mental state in which a person is deeply focused and less aware of their surroundings.
Suggestion (noun)An idea or instruction given to someone to influence how they think, feel, or act.
Conscious (adjective)Awake, aware of your surroundings, and able to think and make choices.
Gullible (adjective)Too quick to believe everything other people say; easily fooled.
Cliché (noun)An idea, phrase, or image that has been used so many times it is no longer original.

Final Wrap-Up Questions

  • Which myth about hypnosis surprised you the most today?
  • Do you think the mind has the power to reduce physical pain, or does pain only depend on the body?
  • Where do you think we get most of our false beliefs—from movies, social media, or word of mouth?
