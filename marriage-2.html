Useful Vocabulary for Your Discussion

Cohabitation (noun)Living together in a romantic relationship without being legally married.
Companionship (noun)The warm feeling of friendship and closeness from spending time with someone you care about.
Spouse (noun)A formal word for a person's husband or wife.
Engagement (noun)A formal agreement to get married in the future.
Household chores (noun)Everyday tasks needed to keep a home clean and running, such as cooking, washing dishes, and cleaning.
Generation (noun)All the people born and living at about the same time, such as parents versus their children.
