Sources and useful context

The figures below are taken from research organisations and official statistical sources. Dates and populations differ: a national survey should not be interpreted as a global statistic.

  1. OECD, Society at a Glance 2024 – Marriage and divorce. OECD averages and comparisons, including ages at first marriage and same-sex marriage statistics.
  2. Pew Research Center (2019) – The landscape of marriage and cohabitation in the U.S.
  3. Pew Research Center (2019) – Why people get married or move in with a partner.
  4. Pew Research Center (2025) – What is the best age to get married? Opinions from adults in 18 countries, not a measure of actual marriage age.
  5. Our World in Data – Marriages and divorces. Global estimates based on United Nations data.
  6. Pew Research Center (2025) – Appendix: Marriage and retirement statistics. Country-level age-at-first-marriage data compiled from the UN; the latest years vary by country.

Language tip: “cohabitation” means living with a romantic partner without being married. “At first marriage” means the first time someone gets married, not a later marriage.
