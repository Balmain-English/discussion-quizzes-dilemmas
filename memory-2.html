Useful Vocabulary for Your Discussion

Recall (verb)To bring a past memory or fact back into your mind.
Cram (verb)To study a huge amount of information in a very short time right before an exam.
Eyewitness (noun)A person who saw an accident or crime happen and can describe it.
Trigger (verb)To cause a memory, feeling, or reaction to start suddenly.
Vivid (adjective)Producing very clear, bright, and detailed images in your mind.
Long-term (adjective)Lasting for a long period of time in the future.

Final Wrap-Up Questions

  • Do you think smartphones and search engines are making our memories weaker, or just freeing our brains for other things?
  • If you could permanently erase one embarrassing or unpleasant memory, would you do it? Why or why not?
  • What is one memory from the past year that you hope you will remember for the rest of your life?
