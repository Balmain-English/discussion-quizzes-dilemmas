Useful Vocabulary for Your Discussion

Myth (noun)A popular belief or story that many people think is true, even though it is false.
First impression (noun)The immediate opinion you form about someone the first time you meet them.
Multitask (verb)To try to do two or more tasks at the exact same time.
Recharge (verb)To rest and get your energy back after feeling tired or stressed.
Bias (noun)A natural tendency to prefer one idea or side over another, often without realizing it.
Trait (noun)A particular quality in someone's personality, such as patience or curiosity.
