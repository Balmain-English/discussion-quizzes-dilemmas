Useful Vocabulary for Your Discussion

Atmosphere (noun)The layer of gases surrounding the Earth or another planet.
Air resistance (noun)The force of air pushing against a moving object and slowing it down.
Orbit (noun)The curved path that a planet, moon, or satellite follows around a larger object in space.
Scatter (verb)To throw or spread things in many different directions.
Vibration (noun)A very fast, small shaking movement that creates sound.
With the naked eye (phrase)Seeing something without using glasses, a telescope, or a microscope.

Final Wrap-Up Questions

  • Did you learn any of these science myths in school when you were younger?
  • Do you think space exploration is worth the money, or should governments spend more on solving problems on Earth?
  • What scientific discovery or invention in the last 100 years do you think changed human life the most?
