Useful Vocabulary for Your Discussion

Prototype (noun)The first working model of a new invention, built to test how it works.
Data center (noun)A large building filled with thousands of powerful computers that store and manage online information.
Wireless (adjective)Sending signals or connecting devices without using physical cables or wires.
Browser (noun)A computer program or app used to visit websites, such as Chrome, Safari, or Firefox.
Recover (verb)To get back something that was lost, stolen, or accidentally deleted.
Catchy (adjective)Easy to remember and pleasant to hear, often used for names, slogans, or songs.
