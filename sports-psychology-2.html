Useful Vocabulary for Your Discussion

Choke (verb)To fail to perform well under pressure when you would normally succeed.
Visualization (noun)Creating a clear mental picture in your mind of something you want to happen.
Fatigue (noun)Extreme physical or mental tiredness.
Routine (noun)A regular sequence of actions that you follow in the same way every time.
Endurance (noun)The ability to keep doing a difficult physical activity for a long time.
Podium (noun)A small raised platform where the top three winners stand to receive their medals.

Final Wrap-Up Questions
